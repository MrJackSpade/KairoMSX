# v1.4.0

The first release of KairoMSX, an MSX emulator.

- Plays MSX, MSX2 and MSX turbo R games, with as much RAM as the machine safely allows by default.
- A catalog of MSX games with descriptions and artwork, with every multi-disk set grouped as one game.
- The D-pad is the cursor keys and the joystick by default, in both controller layouts, and Space and Return are on B and A.
- Mappings follow a cascade: the MSX defaults, your all-games mapping, the game's catalog mapping, then your own for the game.
- Scaling anchor: Settings › Graphics pins the picture to any of nine places on the screen, separately for landscape and portrait, and each game's settings can choose its own. Pick it from a 3x3 grid.
- On a two-screen device (RG DS), the keyboard, joystick and touchpad are on the bottom screen, and the D-pad moves between the screens in the library.

Both APK variants and the Play bundle have the same features.
